🏪 Ghost Market Rules & Policies

Last Updated: April 5, 2026
About Ghost Market: Ghost Market is ZeusChat's anonymous peer-to-peer marketplace. Buyers and sellers transact using their Zeus-PINs, maintaining their Ghost Mode privacy. All sales are protected by our escrow system and physical delivery is conducted through Pudo Lockers for maximum anonymity.

1. Who Can Sell on Ghost Market

Selling on Ghost Market is exclusively available to verified ZeusChat subscribers:

  • Pro subscribers and Teams subscribers may apply to become sellers
  • Free tier users may browse and buy, but may not list items for sale
  • All seller applications are subject to admin review and approval
  • All sellers must have completed KYC verification
  • ZeusChat reserves the right to revoke seller status for policy violations

To become a seller, upgrade your subscription and submit a seller application from the Ghost Market page.

2. Prohibited Items

The following are strictly prohibited on Ghost Market. Listing prohibited items will result in immediate item removal, seller status suspension, and may result in permanent account ban:

🚫 Absolutely Prohibited

  • Illegal goods, substances, or services of any kind
  • Drugs, narcotics, controlled substances, or drug paraphernalia
  • Weapons, firearms, ammunition, or dangerous explosives
  • Counterfeit, pirated, or fraudulently obtained goods
  • Stolen property or goods obtained through criminal activity
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any material exploiting minors
  • Human trafficking services or related materials
  • Malware, hacking tools, or services designed to compromise computer systems
  • Personal data, account credentials, or access to other people's accounts
  • Fraudulent documents, fake IDs, or fake credentials
  • Currency, banknotes, or financial instruments (unless seller is a licensed financial service provider)
  • Prescription medicines or unlicensed healthcare products
  • Endangered species or illegal wildlife products
  • Tobacco products, e-cigarettes, or alcohol
  • Adult content, pornographic material, or sexual services

✅ Examples of Allowed Items

  • Electronics and gadgets (new or secondhand)
  • Clothing, fashion accessories, and footwear
  • Books, physical media, and collectibles
  • Handmade crafts and art
  • Household items and furniture (deliverable via Pudo)
  • Digital products and services (within acceptable use)
  • Sports and outdoor equipment
  • General merchandise and everyday goods

ZeusChat reserves the right to determine, at its sole discretion, whether an item is prohibited. When in doubt, contact us before listing: marketplace@zeustechafrica.com

3. Seller Responsibilities

Approved sellers agree to the following obligations:

  • List only items you legally own and have the right to sell
  • Provide accurate, honest, and complete descriptions of all items
  • Include clear, real photographs of the actual item being sold
  • Accurately state the condition of the item (new, used, refurbished)
  • Dispatch confirmed orders via Pudo Locker within 3 business days
  • Respond to buyer messages and disputes within 48 hours
  • Not attempt to conduct transactions outside the Ghost Market escrow system
  • Not solicit or share personal contact details with buyers to bypass the marketplace

4. Buyer Protection and Escrow System

Ghost Market uses a built-in escrow payment system to protect buyers from fraud:

  1. Buyer pays into escrow — Payment is held securely by ZeusChat and not released to the seller until delivery is confirmed.
  2. Seller dispatches item — The seller drops the item at a Pudo Locker and provides the collection PIN to the buyer via the marketplace.
  3. Buyer collects item — Buyer collects from the Pudo Locker and confirms receipt in the app within 5 days of the dispatch notification.
  4. Payment released — Once the buyer confirms receipt, the escrowed payment is released to the seller.
  5. Auto-release — If the buyer does not confirm or dispute within 5 days of the dispatch notification, payment is automatically released to the seller.

Buyer Guarantee: If an item is significantly not as described or was not received, buyers may open a dispute within the 5-day window to trigger a refund review. See Section 5 for dispute resolution.

5. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute between buyer and seller:

  1. The buyer opens a dispute through the Ghost Market order page within 5 days of the dispatch notification
  2. The seller is notified and has 48 hours to respond with evidence (photos, tracking, etc.)
  3. A ZeusChat marketplace moderator reviews the dispute within 3–5 business days
  4. The moderator issues a decision: full refund to buyer, partial refund, or payment release to seller
  5. The decision of the moderator is final

Grounds for a successful buyer dispute include:

  • Item significantly not as described in the listing
  • Item never received and seller cannot provide proof of dispatch
  • Item received was damaged, broken, or unusable
  • Counterfeit or fraudulent item received

6. Pudo Locker Delivery

All Ghost Market physical deliveries must be conducted via Pudo Locker to maintain buyer and seller anonymity:

  • Sellers must use the Pudo network to drop off sold items
  • Direct hand-to-hand deliveries or courier deliveries requiring personal details are not permitted through Ghost Market
  • The cost of Pudo delivery is the responsibility of the seller unless otherwise stated in the listing
  • Buyers collect from any Pudo Locker location using the collection PIN provided by the seller
  • Sellers must provide Pudo tracking/dispatch confirmation within 3 business days of order confirmation

For digital goods, delivery is by electronic means within the app messaging system.

8. Anonymity Rules

Ghost Market is designed to protect the anonymity of both buyers and sellers:

  • Users are identified only by their Zeus-PIN during marketplace transactions
  • You must not ask for, share, or attempt to discover the personal identity of other marketplace participants
  • Reverse-engineering a user's identity from available information is a violation of these rules
  • Sharing another user's identity, Zeus-PIN, or transaction details outside the platform is prohibited
  • Pudo Locker is used specifically to avoid the exchange of physical addresses

9. Policy Enforcement

Violations of Ghost Market policies may result in:

  • Immediate removal of the offending listing
  • Warning issued to the seller's account
  • Temporary suspension of seller privileges
  • Permanent revocation of seller status
  • Account suspension or permanent ban
  • Reporting to relevant law enforcement authorities (for illegal items)
