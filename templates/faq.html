Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for serious users.

These are the questions most people ask before joining or upgrading inside the Ghost Town.

Do I need a phone number?

No. ZeusChat is built around Zeus-PIN identity, not phone-number discovery.

Why is there approval?

Approval helps keep the network human and reduces fraud, bot abuse, and impersonation.

How does Ghost Market protect buyers?

Escrow holds funds until the buyer confirms receipt, then releases payment to the seller.

How do creators earn money?

Paid post unlocks credit the creator wallet, with creators keeping 80% of paid post revenue.

Can messages disappear?

Yes. ZeusChat supports TTL-based messaging and delete-everywhere workflows.

Where can I get more help?

Use the Help Center or contact support@zeustechafrica.com.
