About ZeusChat

Private communication, built in Africa.

ZeusChat exists because privacy, identity, and digital commerce should not be gated behind phone-number exposure or surveillance-heavy platforms.

Our story

We started ZeusChat to remove the biggest friction in secure communication: forced identity exposure. In too many markets, privacy tools are either imported, expensive, or culturally detached from how people actually communicate.

ZeusChat replaces the phone number with Zeus-PIN identity, combines admin verification with anonymity, and brings commerce plus community into the same trusted network.

Our mission

Build the most trusted privacy platform on Earth, starting with Africa.

That means strong verification without public exposure, monetization for creators without platform exploitation, and products that respect local realities like bandwidth cost, mobile-first usage, and multilingual communities.
