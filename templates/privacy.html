🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026
Summary: ZeusChat is built on a "Ghost Mode" privacy-first principle. We collect only what is necessary to operate the Service. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising purposes. This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

1. Who We Are

ZeusTech Africa (Pty) Ltd ("ZeusChat", "we", "us", "our") is the responsible party / data controller for personal information processed through the ZeusChat application. We are registered in South Africa.

Contact: privacy@zeustechafrica.com

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

2.1 Account Registration Data

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth (to verify age eligibility)
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — never in plaintext)
  • Zeus-PIN (your unique anonymous identifier)

2.2 KYC Verification Data

  • Government-issued ID document image (Passport or National ID)
  • Selfie photograph for facial verification
  • Nationality and address (as appearing on ID document)

2.3 Usage and Technical Data

  • IP address (for security and fraud prevention)
  • Device type and operating system
  • Login timestamps and session data
  • App feature usage (aggregated, non-personal)

2.4 Messaging Data

  • Messages you send and receive through the App
  • Media files (images, documents) shared in chats
  • Message status metadata (sent, delivered, read timestamps)

2.5 Payment Data

  • Subscription tier and payment status
  • Transaction reference numbers
  • Note: Full payment card details are processed directly by PayFast and are not stored by ZeusChat

4. KYC Data Handling

KYC (Know Your Customer) data is subject to heightened protection:

  • KYC documents are stored in an access-controlled, encrypted storage environment
  • Only authorised administrators for the purpose of verification review KYC data
  • KYC documents are not shared with other users under any circumstances
  • Approved KYC documents are retained for 5 years to comply with South African financial regulation (FICA)
  • Rejected KYC submissions are deleted within 90 days of rejection

For full details, see our KYC Verification Policy.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR and POPIA

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email: privacy@zeustechafrica.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • PayFast: Payment processing. PayFast receives sufficient transaction data to process payments. See PayFast's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting / Infrastructure: Our servers and infrastructure providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred to the successor entity under the same privacy protections.

No personal information is shared with advertisers or marketing companies.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

ZeusChat uses essential session cookies and browser localStorage to maintain your login session and application preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

9. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords stored using bcrypt hashing with salt
  • Access-controlled database with least-privilege principles
  • Session management with secure, HTTP-only cookies
  • Regular security reviews and audits

10. International Data Transfers

ZeusChat is operated from South Africa. If you access the Service from outside South Africa, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in South Africa. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for any cross-border data transfers.

11. Children's Privacy

ZeusChat is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under 18. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a minor, we will delete that information immediately. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us at privacy@zeustechafrica.com.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or email. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the Policy was last revised. Continued use of ZeusChat after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
