🪪 KYC Verification Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026

1. Why KYC is Required

ZeusChat requires all users to complete a Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification process before accessing the platform. KYC verification is mandatory for the following reasons:

  • Legal Compliance: The Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) and related South African legislation require identity verification for platforms that facilitate financial transactions.
  • Safety of our Community: KYC prevents impersonation, fraud, fake accounts, and bad actors from accessing the platform.
  • Ghost Market Integrity: Real-world transactions via the Ghost Market require verified participants to protect buyers and sellers.
  • Age Verification: To enforce our 18+ age requirement and protect minors.
  • Subscription and Payment Integrity: To prevent subscription fraud and unauthorised payment activity.

While ZeusChat uses a "Ghost Mode" PIN system to protect your privacy from other users, internal identity verification is necessary to operate a safe and compliant platform.

2. What Documents Are Accepted

We accept the following government-issued identity documents:

🛂 Passport Accepted

Any valid, unexpired national passport. The photo page must be clearly visible and not expired.

🪪 South African National ID Card (Smart ID) Accepted

The green barcoded ID book or the new smart ID card issued by the Department of Home Affairs.

🪪 National ID Card (International) Accepted

National identity cards issued by any recognised sovereign government. Must show full name, photo, and date of birth.

🚗 Driver's Licence Not Accepted

Driver's licences are not accepted as KYC documents at this time.

🏠 Utility Bills / Bank Statements Not Accepted (standalone)

Utility bills or bank statements alone are not sufficient. They may be requested as supplementary proof in exceptional cases.

3. Document Requirements

To be accepted, your submitted documents must meet the following requirements:

  • The document must be valid and not expired
  • All four corners of the document must be visible
  • The image must be clear, in focus, and well-lit
  • Text, photo, and security features must be legible
  • No part of the document may be covered, obscured, or cropped
  • Digital editing or alteration of documents is strictly prohibited and may result in permanent account ban
  • File formats accepted: JPEG, PNG, PDF (max 10 MB per file)

4. The Selfie / Liveness Check

In addition to your identity document, you must submit a selfie photograph for facial verification. Requirements:

  • Your face must be clearly visible and centred in the frame
  • Your face must match the photo on the submitted identity document
  • No hats, sunglasses, or face coverings
  • Photo must be taken in good lighting (no backlighting)
  • The selfie must be a live photo — screenshots or photos of photos are not accepted

5. How We Verify

KYC verification is conducted through a combination of automated checks and manual review by authorised ZeusChat administrators:

  1. You upload your identity document and selfie through the secure KYC upload form
  2. Our system performs initial automated format and quality checks
  3. A trained administrator reviews your submission for authenticity and completeness
  4. You receive a notification of approval or rejection within 24–48 hours
  5. Upon approval, your account is fully activated

6. Data Protection Measures

Your KYC documents are treated with the highest level of protection:

  • Documents are stored in a dedicated, access-restricted, encrypted file store
  • Only authorised administrators can access KYC documents, and only for the purpose of verification
  • Access to KYC documents is logged and audited
  • Documents are never shared with other users or third parties (except where legally required)
  • All transmission of KYC data occurs over HTTPS/TLS

See our Privacy Policy for full data handling details.

7. Rejection and Appeal Process

If your KYC submission is rejected, you will receive a notification stating the reason. Common rejection reasons include:

  • Blurry or unreadable document image
  • Expired document
  • Document not fully visible
  • Selfie does not match document photo
  • Suspected digital manipulation

After receiving a rejection notice, you may:

  1. Re-submit your KYC with a corrected document or clearer images through your account settings
  2. Submit up to 3 times before a manual review is triggered
  3. If you believe your rejection was in error, contact us at kyc@zeustechafrica.com with your Zeus-PIN and the reason you believe the rejection was incorrect

Appeals are reviewed within 3–5 business days.

8. Retention Period

KYC data is retained in accordance with South African legal requirements:

  • Approved KYC documents: Retained for 5 years from the date of verification, in compliance with FICA requirements
  • Rejected KYC submissions: Deleted within 90 days of rejection
  • Upon account deletion: KYC data is retained for the legally required period, then securely deleted

9. Contact

For KYC-related queries, appeals, or complaints:
Email: kyc@zeustechafrica.com
Response time: 1–3 business days

KYC verification is mandatory for all ZeusChat users. Submission of false, altered, or fraudulent documents constitutes a criminal offence and will result in immediate and permanent account termination and may be reported to law enforcement.
