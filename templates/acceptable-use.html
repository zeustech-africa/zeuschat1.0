⚖️ Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the standards of conduct expected of all ZeusChat users. It supplements our Terms of Service and applies to all use of the ZeusChat platform, including messaging, Ghost Market, and all other features.

1. Prohibited Content

You must not create, send, share, upload, or otherwise distribute any content that falls within the categories below.

1.1 Hate Speech and Discrimination

🚫

Content that dehumanises or promotes hatred

Content that attacks, demeans, or incites hatred against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

🚫

Slurs and derogatory language

Use of slurs or derogatory terms to attack individuals or groups based on protected characteristics.

1.2 Harassment and Cyberbullying

🚫

Direct harassment

Repeated, unwanted contact that is designed to distress, intimidate, or harm another individual.

🚫

Threats of violence

Any content that threatens physical harm or violence against any person or group.

🚫

Doxxing

Publishing or threatening to publish private personal information of another person without their consent.

🚫

Non-consensual intimate images

Sharing intimate or sexual images or videos of another person without their explicit consent.

1.3 Illegal Content

🚫

Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)

Any content that sexually exploits or depicts minors. This will be reported to the South African Police Service (SAPS) and the relevant child protection authority immediately.

🚫

Content facilitating illegal activity

Instructions, plans, or coordination for criminal activities, including but not limited to drug trafficking, human trafficking, terrorism, or financial fraud.

🚫

Extremist or terrorist content

Content glorifying, inciting, or promoting terrorism, mass violence, or violent extremism.

1.4 Spam and Inauthentic Behaviour

🚫

Spam and unsolicited mass messages

Sending bulk, repetitive, or unsolicited messages to users who have not requested them.

🚫

Impersonation

Claiming to be ZeusChat staff, a government authority, or another user in a deceptive manner.

🚫

Phishing and social engineering

Attempting to trick users into revealing passwords, Zeus-PINs, or other sensitive information.

1.5 Platform Security

🚫

Hacking and unauthorised access

Attempting to gain unauthorised access to ZeusChat systems, other user accounts, or related infrastructure.

🚫

Malware distribution

Uploading or distributing viruses, ransomware, spyware, or any other malicious software.

🚫

Denial of service attacks

Actions designed to overload or disrupt ZeusChat infrastructure or other users' access to the Service.

2. Enforcement Actions

ZeusChat takes all violations of this AUP seriously. Enforcement actions are proportionate to the severity of the violation.

Severity Example Violations Enforcement Action
Critical CSAM, terrorism content, human trafficking, severe identity fraud Immediate permanent account ban without warning; mandatory reporting to law enforcement
High Threats of violence, doxxing, distribution of malware, phishing Immediate account suspension; permanent ban on second offence; potential law enforcement referral
Medium Harassment, hate speech, impersonation, Ghost Market policy violations Formal warning + content removal; 7-day suspension on second offence; permanent ban on third offence
Low Minor spam, minor listing violations Informal warning + content removal; escalation if repeated

ZeusChat reserves the right to take any enforcement action at its sole discretion, including actions not listed above, depending on the circumstances of the violation.

3. Appeals Process

If your account has been suspended or banned and you believe the action was taken in error, you may appeal:

  1. Submit your appeal to appeals@zeustechafrica.com within 14 days of the enforcement action
  2. Include your Zeus-PIN and a clear explanation of why you believe the action was taken in error
  3. Include any relevant evidence supporting your appeal
  4. Our trust and safety team will review your appeal within 5 business days
  5. The decision on appeal is final

Note: Appeals are not available for accounts banned for CSAM, terrorism, or human trafficking offences.

4. Reporting Violations

How to Report a Violation

If you encounter content or behaviour that violates this Acceptable Use Policy, please report it:

  • In-app: Use the "Report" button on any message or user profile
  • Email: safety@zeustechafrica.com
  • Urgent / CSAM reports: csam@zeustechafrica.com (dedicated, monitored 24/7)

All reports are reviewed by our trust and safety team. Reports are kept confidential. False reports made in bad faith may themselves result in enforcement action.

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation

ZeusChat will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations where required by South African law or applicable international agreements. We will disclose user information to law enforcement only in response to lawful legal process or as required to prevent imminent harm to any person.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or to report a violation:
General safety: safety@zeustechafrica.com
Account appeals: appeals@zeustechafrica.com
CSAM reports: csam@zeustechafrica.com

This Acceptable Use Policy is incorporated by reference into the ZeusChat Terms of Service. Use of ZeusChat constitutes acceptance of this AUP.
