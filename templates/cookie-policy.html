🍪 Cookie Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026
Short version: ZeusChat uses only essential cookies required to make the app work. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. We do not share cookie data with third parties.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or application places on your device to remember information about your visit. They serve various purposes, from keeping you logged in to tracking your browsing behaviour across websites.

ZeusChat also uses browser localStorage — a similar technology that stores data locally on your device — for application preferences and session state.

2. Cookies We Use

ZeusChat uses only strictly essential cookies and local storage. We use no third-party, advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies of any kind.

Name / Key Type Purpose Duration
session Essential (session cookie) Maintains your login session. Required for authentication. Contains a secure session identifier — not your personal data. 30 days (persistent login) or until browser close (if not using persistent login)
csrf_token Essential (security cookie) Cross-Site Request Forgery protection. Ensures form submissions originate from ZeusChat pages. Session duration
localStorage: theme Functional (local storage) Remembers your display theme preference (if applicable). Persistent (until manually cleared)
localStorage: notification_prefs Functional (local storage) Stores your notification preferences locally to reduce server requests. Persistent (until manually cleared)

3. What We Do NOT Use

✅ ZeusChat does NOT use any of the following:
  • Third-party advertising cookies (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Pixel)
  • Cross-site tracking cookies
  • Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar)
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Behavioural targeting or profiling cookies
  • Performance cookies that track your browsing habits

4. Why We Cannot Disable Essential Cookies

The session cookie is strictly necessary for ZeusChat to function. Without it, you cannot:

  • Log in to your account
  • Stay authenticated across pages
  • Use any features that require an account

Because these cookies are essential to the service you have requested, we do not require your consent to set them. All other (non-essential) cookies require your prior consent, but since we do not use any non-essential cookies, no consent prompt is needed.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Here is how to manage cookies in the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and Other Site Data
  • Safari (iOS / macOS): Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions → Cookies and Site Data
  • Samsung Internet: Settings → Privacy → Smart Anti-Tracking

Note: Deleting or blocking the ZeusChat session cookie will log you out of the application and you will need to log in again.

6. Clearing localStorage

To clear locally stored ZeusChat preferences (localStorage):

  1. Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12 or right-click → Inspect)
  2. Navigate to the "Application" or "Storage" tab
  3. Select "Local Storage" → find the ZeusChat domain
  4. Delete individual keys or clear all entries

Alternatively, you can clear all site data through your browser's settings under "Clear Browsing Data".

7. Third-Party Payment Cookies

When you proceed to payment through PayFast, you will be redirected to the PayFast payment gateway. PayFast may set its own cookies on their domain. ZeusChat has no control over PayFast's cookies. Please review PayFast's Privacy Policy for details on their cookie usage.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new types of cookies or change our cookie practices, we will update this policy and notify users via in-app notification. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
