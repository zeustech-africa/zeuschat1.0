💸 Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 5, 2026

ZeusChat is committed to a fair and transparent refund process. This policy covers all payments made through ZeusChat, including subscriptions, one-off purchases, and Ghost Market transactions.

1. Subscription Refunds

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee ✓ Eligible

If you are not satisfied with your ZeusChat Pro or Teams subscription, you may request a full refund within 7 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase.

This guarantee applies to first-time purchases only — subsequent renewals or upgrades are not covered by the 7-day guarantee.

Renewals and Auto-Renew Charges Conditional

Subscription renewals are charged automatically at the start of each billing period. If you were charged for a renewal that you did not intend:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of the renewal charge
  • Refunds for renewals are considered on a case-by-case basis
  • You will need to provide evidence that you had cancelled or intended to cancel before the renewal date

Cancellations After 7 Days Not Eligible

Subscription payments older than 7 days are non-refundable. You may cancel your subscription at any time through Settings to prevent future charges, but the current billing period is non-refundable.

After cancellation, your subscription features remain active until the end of the paid period.

2. One-Off Payment Refunds

Feature Unlocks and One-Off Purchases Not Eligible

One-off payments for specific features, add-ons, or unlockable functionality are non-refundable once the feature has been activated or accessed. This applies to:

  • Profile customisation purchases
  • Any feature unlock payments outside of a subscription

If a feature fails to activate due to a technical error on our side, please contact support and a full refund or credit will be issued.

3. Ghost Market Transaction Refunds

Escrow Protection Protected

All Ghost Market purchases are protected by our escrow payment system. Your payment is held securely and not released to the seller until you confirm receipt.

Item Not Received Refund Eligible

If a seller fails to dispatch your order within 3 business days and you have not received a dispatch notification, you may open a dispute for a full refund.

Item Significantly Not as Described Refund Eligible

If the item you receive is significantly different from the listing description (wrong item, major undisclosed damage, counterfeit), you may open a dispute within 5 days of receiving the Pudo dispatch notification.

Change of Mind Not Eligible

Ghost Market does not support change-of-mind refunds. Once an order is confirmed by the seller and dispatched, you are committed to the purchase.

Ghost Market refunds are subject to the dispute resolution process outlined in the Ghost Market Policy.

4. Exceptions and Special Circumstances

Refunds may be considered outside the standard policy in exceptional circumstances, including:

  • Service outages lasting more than 24 hours during your billing period
  • Confirmed technical billing errors on our part
  • Unauthorised charges resulting from a security breach of our systems
  • Death or serious medical incapacitation of the account holder (documentation required)

For exceptional circumstances, contact billing@zeustechafrica.com with full details.

5. How to Request a Refund

1
Email billing@zeustechafrica.com with the subject line: "Refund Request – [Your Zeus-PIN]"
2
Include your Zeus-PIN, the payment amount, the date of payment, and the reason for the refund request.
3
Our team will acknowledge your request within 1 business day and may request supporting information.
4
If approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

6. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved:

  • Approval notification: Within 1–3 business days of submission
  • Refund processing by PayFast: 3–5 business days after approval
  • Bank / card reflection: Up to 5–10 business days depending on your bank

Refunds are returned to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.

7. Contact

For all refund and billing queries:

Response time: 1 business day
